Act I — Roots · 1925–1939

We did not begin in a city. We began in nature.

Before the journeys began, generations lived close to the earth in Padinjarangadi — a scatter of coconut groves, paddy fields and Nalukettu courtyards deep in rural Kerala. Life moved to the rhythm of the monsoon and the temple lamp, and the family kept watch over the sacred Pambinkavu, the serpent grove where the forest was left wild and untouched.

The great-grandfather was not remembered as a hero, but as something quieter and larger: a keeper of the land. Into this world of brass bells and butterflies, of wind moving through the palms, my grandfather Thami was born.

"Custodians of a grove that still breathes — this is where the century starts."
